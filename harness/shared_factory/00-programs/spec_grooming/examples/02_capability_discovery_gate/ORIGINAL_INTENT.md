# Original Intent: Capability Discovery Gate

## Raw Capture

Before creating new specs or programs, the groomer should research existing
programs, workflows, automations, skills, docs, trackers, and Notion pages so it
extends the right owner instead of creating duplicate work.

## Intent Anchors

- Discovery must happen before polished spec creation.
- Evidence must name the surfaces searched.
- The packet must record why the selected owner is correct.
